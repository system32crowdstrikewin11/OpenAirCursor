OpenAirCursor turns your front camera into a touchless, dual-hand spatial pointer for Android—running 100% offline with zero network permissions, zero trackers, and zero Google Play Services.

Show one hand to spawn a single cursor, or raise both hands for two independent cursors and mid-air pinch-to-zoom.

Features:
* On-device 21-point dual-hand tracking (MediaPipe Hand Landmarker)
* One-Euro filter + display-rate interpolation with live Sensitivity and Smoothness tuning
* Optional floating corner camera preview with active tracking zone & mirror toggle
* Zero INTERNET permission and zero telemetry

Gestures:
* 1 or 2 hands: Spawns 1 cursor (Cyan) or 2 cursors (Cyan + Magenta)
* Thumb + Index: Quick pinch = Tap | Double pinch = Double click | Drag = Swipe | Hold = Long press
* Both hands pinch: Pinch-to-Zoom
* Thumb + Middle: Quick pinch = Back
* Thumb + Ring: Quick pinch = Home
* Thumb + Pinky: Quick pinch = Recents
