OpenAirCursor v1.0.6

- Improved keyboard typing accuracy by tightening double-tap snap radius to 26dp, speeding up tap strokes to 35ms, and raising long-press threshold to 720ms.
- Kept fast 1.0.3 tracking smoothness alongside landscape rotation support.
